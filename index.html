    How it works

    From idea to metal in four moves.

    1. 01

      Send your idea

      A sketch, a photo, a 3D file or a physical model. Tell us the metal, karat and quantity.

    2. 02

      Model & print

      We build or refine the CAD and print it in castable wax or resin. You approve before anything is poured.

    3. 03

      Cast

      Your piece is cast in the metal you chose, with the precision three decades of daily pours have taught us.

    4. 04

      Finish & ship

      Cleaned, finished to spec, and on its way — across Manhattan or across the world.
